Tabs, for when one window is enough.

- Turn on "Open files and folders in tabs" in Settings → Windows, and opening a new file or folder joins your current window as a tab instead of opening a new one
- Drag a tab out to give it its own window, and merge windows back together from the Window menu
- Off by default: nothing changes unless you turn it on
